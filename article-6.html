The Asset

Manage your health like a balance sheet

You already know how to read a company. Your body works the same way.

3 min read · Updated Nov 2026

You wouldn't run a business without a balance sheet. You wouldn't invest without tracking returns. Yet most people run their body — the one asset they can't buy back — on vibes.

Income, expenses, and net worth

Think of sleep, strength, and glucose control as income. Stress, sugar, and poor recovery are expenses. Your biological age — the number from the assessment — is your net worth. The goal isn't to feel "healthy"; it's to make the number move in your favor.

The three-line audit

  • Strength — the engine. Are you protecting it?
  • Blood sugar — the compounding fee. Is it quietly draining you?
  • Sleep — the repair line. Is it actually charging you?

Measure quarterly, not monthly

Bloodwork you can't see changes weekly. Once a quarter: the executive panel from the checkup article, a week of glucose data, and a re-take of the assessment. That's your board meeting.

What gets measured gets managed. The wealthy understand this about money; almost no one applies it to the body.

How it ties back
