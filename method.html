A simpler way to think about aging

Three levers, in the right order. Everything here follows from them.

3 min read · Updated Nov 2026

Most health advice fails because it treats symptoms in isolation. We take a different position: your body is a single system, and it responds to three levers — in a specific order.

The three levers, in order

  • Strength first — muscle is the body's largest metabolic engine and its densest concentration of mitochondria. Keep the muscle, and you keep the metabolism.
  • Then blood sugar — controlling glucose slows glycation, protects your telomeres, and stops the energy crashes.
  • Then sleep — deep sleep is when repair happens. Without it, the first two levers don't stick.

Why the order matters

Most people reverse it: they diet and do cardio first, then wonder why they're exhausted. Strength protects the metabolism that dieting and cardio would otherwise destroy. Fix the order, and the rest gets easier.

Who this is for

People who've already won the visible game and want to win the invisible one: energy, longevity, and a body that keeps compounding. Not everyone needs this — which is exactly the point.

Nothing on this site is medical advice. It's a framework — a way to think clearly about the one asset you can't buy back.
