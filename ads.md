# Social Media Ads — EQUILIBRIA · Private Health

Eight hooks designed to pull high-net-worth, health-conscious people onto the landing page (`index.html`). Each ad is a teaser: it opens a curiosity gap and lets the CTA finish the job.

---

## 1. Curiosity / Mystery
**Headline:** This content was never meant to appear in your feed.
**Body:** 99% of what you scroll past was made for everyone. The other 1% lives here.
**CTA:** See the 1% →
**Trigger:** curiosity + scarcity · Instagram / X / TikTok

## 2. Identity / Gap
**Headline:** You'll spend $300k on a watch, but still look for answers in free groups.
**Body:** What separates you was never the purchase — it's the table you sit at. No "community" here. Just a few.
**CTA:** See who's here →
**Trigger:** status + gap · LinkedIn / X

## 3. Time Efficiency
**Headline:** Your time is more expensive than you think.
**Body:** You'll give a dinner three hours, yet let an algorithm random-feed your information. Compress what matters into ten minutes a day.
**CTA:** Enter — for people who value their time →
**Trigger:** time scarcity · LinkedIn

## 4. Information Edge
**Headline:** The people who see it first and the people who understand it were never the same group.
**Body:** Public information belongs to everyone. The part that matters is never public. This is one step ahead of the headlines.
**CTA:** See what others missed →
**Trigger:** information asymmetry · X

## 5. Contrarian / Resonance
**Headline:** Truly wealthy people rarely say "money is no object."
**Body:** They don't care about price — they care about whether it's worth it. If that sounds like you, this may be to your taste.
**CTA:** Enter →
**Trigger:** contrarian + resonance · Instagram

## 6. Gate / Scarcity
**Headline:** This page is open to very few.
**Body:** No ad slots, no trends, no $9 funnels. We do one thing: get found by the right people.
**CTA:** Check whether you're one of them →
**Trigger:** gate + scarcity · TikTok / YouTube

## 7. Private Backdoor
**Headline:** What you're most curious about is what others least want to share.
**Body:** What's public was always meant for everyone. The useful part is never public. There's a back door here.
**CTA:** Take the back door →
**Trigger:** privacy + curiosity · X

## 8. Direction / Foresight
**Headline:** You're not short on capital. You're short on where the next five years go.
**Body:** Rather than burn two hours in a feed, spend five minutes seeing how a few people read what's next.
**CTA:** Five minutes, worth it → Enter
**Trigger:** foresight + efficiency · LinkedIn / YouTube

---

**How to use:** run ads 1–3 first (curiosity, identity, and time convert best with this audience). Point every CTA at the landing page (`index.html`). Keep the imagery dark, minimal, and gold — this audience is repelled by anything that looks like a "sale."
