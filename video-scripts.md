# Short-Video Scripts — EQUILIBRIA · Private Health

Two 15–30s scripts for TikTok / Reels / Shorts. Style: dark, gold, minimal — no "sale" energy. B-roll can be stock or AI-generated; keep text overlays in a thin serif on a dark field.

---

## Script A — from Ad #2 (Identity / Gap)
**Length:** ~20s · **Aspect:** 9:16 · **Mood:** quiet status play

| Time | Visual | Text overlay / Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Extreme close-up of a luxury watch, gold light | "You'll spend $300k on a watch…" |
| 3–8s | Cut: hand scrolling generic content on a phone, fast cuts | "…but still look for answers in free groups." |
| 8–14s | Slow push-in on a dark, private, empty table | "What separates you was never the purchase — it's the table you sit at." |
| 14–18s | Logo mark over black | "EQUILIBRIA · Private Health" |
| 18–20s | CTA lower-third | "See who's here →" |

---

## Script B — from Ad #6 (Gate / Scarcity)
**Length:** ~18s · **Aspect:** 9:16 · **Mood:** exclusive, almost unreachable

| Time | Visual | Text overlay / Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Black screen, single gold line appears | "This page is open to very few." |
| 3–9s | Slow reveals: no ad slots, no trends, no "$9 funnels" — each as a struck-through line | "No ad slots. No trends. No $9 funnels." |
| 9–14s | Gold hexagon mark assembles (matches the favicon) | "We do one thing: get found by the right people." |
| 14–18s | CTA | "Check whether you're one of them →" |

---

**Production notes**
- Sound: low ambient hum or silence; avoid energetic/stocky music.
- Text: Playfair Display (or similar serif), gold `#c8a45c` on `#0d0d0f`.
- Always end with the same logo/CTA lockup for brand recognition.
