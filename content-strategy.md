# Content Strategy — EQUILIBRIA · Private Health

## Positioning (one line)
Health, translated from "wellness" into "asset management": your body as an asset that compounds — anti-aging, energy, and precision, not green juice.

## The audience's real pain points (not the obvious ones)
1. **Anti-aging / longevity** — look younger, live longer.
2. **Energy & performance** — more clear-headed hours per day.
3. **Sleep & stress** — the "silent collapse" of high performers.
4. **Precision prevention** — the risks a routine checkup misses.
5. **Privacy & measurement** — their data, quantified, and no one else's.

## Page structure (landing → click path)
1. **Hook layer** (hero) — deliver the counterintuitive promise, keep half the answer.
2. **Trust layer** — data, authority, cases (they buy proof, not enthusiasm).
3. **Content layer** — columnized articles, each linking to the next.
4. **Interaction layer** — the assessment (quantification is the highest-converting tool).
5. **Private conversion layer** — "for the few", exclusive.

## Topic library (10 titles)
1. Why, after forty-five, your rate of aging is decided by your cells
2. You're not tired because of a lack of sleep
3. Sugar is aging you faster than time
4. The checkup that actually matters
5. Why you wake at 3am
6. The five risks a routine exam can't see
7. Not every "healthy plate" is healthy
8. The silent collapse of high performers
9. Manage your health like a balance sheet
10. Three more clear-headed hours, without caffeine

## 5 mechanisms that make people stay and click
1. **Curiosity gap** — give 70% of the answer, hold the last bit for the next article.
2. **Internal-link hooks** — "if you wake at 3am, this matters more" → a reading path.
3. **Interactive tool** — the assessment → personalized recommendations (highest CTR).
4. **Series** — split one topic into three, with a "next part" teaser.
5. **Scarcity identity** — "open to 200 people" flips the reflex to click.

## User journey (ad → conversion)
Ad → landing hero (counterintuitive hook) → 3-minute assessment → "your body is 4 years older" → recommended articles → exclusive white-paper / private access.
