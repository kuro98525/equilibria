Precision Prevention

The checkup that actually matters

Your annual physical feels thorough. It isn't.

4 min read · Updated Nov 2026

Your annual physical feels thorough. It isn't. It was built to find disease that already exists — not the process that will kill you in twenty years.

The problem with "normal"

Routine panels compare you to a population average, not to optimal. "Normal" fasting glucose, "normal" LDL, "normal" everything — and still the heart attack. Normal is the average of a sick population.

What a routine exam misses

  • ApoB and Lp(a) — the particles that actually drive artery disease. LDL is a proxy; ApoB is the thing itself.
  • Coronary calcium score — a cheap CT scan that shows plaque years before symptoms.
  • Insulin resistance — fasting insulin and HbA1c catch the metabolic slide a decade before diabetes shows up.
  • Inflammation — hs-CRP, the quiet signal behind the process that drives both aging and disease.

The executive checkup, in one line

ApoB + Lp(a), fasting insulin + HbA1c, hs-CRP, a coronary calcium score — and if you want the full picture, two weeks of continuous glucose data.

The principle: measure the process, not the disease. Disease is the endpoint. By the time it appears on a routine panel, the process has been running for years.

How it ties the rest together

This is prevention, not treatment — the layer that turns the three articles before this into a system you can actually track. It's also where the Method becomes a number you can watch.
